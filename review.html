Irregularities noticed in source material

Structure rules have gaps for the specific decision requested: public PDFs state buy-ins, house fees, late-reg timing, and many tournament conditions but may omit the re-entry/add-on clause. We did not interpret that omission as “no re-entry.”

Series calendar can contain overlapping rows: multi-day Day 2 restarts and new events may start around the same time, and a single player cannot be assumed to play both.

Hotel rate language is not a final quote: the page states starting points and qualification requirements, not a fixed nightly price for a particular date/property.

Bellagio official content ambiguity: the page has a dated resumption statement without a year alongside weekly event PDFs. It is recorded as a lead only, not used to claim future dates.

Operator cancellation/change clause: the Venetian structure states management may cancel or change tournaments; recheck close to travel.
