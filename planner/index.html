Headline finding: no tournament is confirmed single-entry. I read all 37 official Venetian structure sheets. None states "single entry only" or prohibits re-entry/rebuy/add-on. The only structure that addresses re-buys is structure_8 (Valuetown Bounty, events #18 & #58), which explicitly allows "subsequent buy in's" — so those two are confirmed not single-entry. The single-entry label is therefore retained as unverified for every other event until the poker room confirms in writing.
Planning limits: No date-specific room rates and no official walking-minute figures were reachable from official sources, so both are blank and flagged. Event finish times are not published, so "play all day" cannot be guaranteed — the day plan gives a primary plus a conditional fallback, never an impossible sequence.

Recommended next move

  1. Ask the Venetian Poker Room to confirm, in writing, the re-entry/rebuy/add-on policy for the one-day events you want.
  2. Pick dates, then price the hotel for those exact dates using the official booking links and the trip-cost calculator.
  3. Re-run change monitoring before nonrefundable payment — schedules, rates and policies change.
