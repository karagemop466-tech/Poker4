Lodging & true cost

Hotels near the tournaments — true cost per night and walking.

True cost per night = room rate + resort fee + 13.38% room tax. Only the Venetian resort fee ($55 + tax) is verified from an official page; other fees and all walking minutes are blank and flagged. Use the ≤30-minute walk filter once walking times are verified.

Dated rate log

Rates are kept as a dated log, not a single value. No official dated room quote has been captured yet, so the log is empty — each verified quote will be appended with its date, source, and timestamp.

To populate: open the property's official booking link for your exact dates, record the quoted room rate, and run python3 tools/check_sources.py (or the scheduled workflow) to append it here with a timestamp. Do not store a single rate as if it applied to all dates.

Walking-route check

Official walking minutes from each hotel to the Venetian poker room were not reachable from an official source, so all walking values are blank and flagged. The ≤30-minute filter is wired and will apply as soon as verified walking times are entered. Verify with Google Maps / Apple Maps and the property's official directions before relying on a walk.
