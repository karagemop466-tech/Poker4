Empty result — by design
No event on the Las Vegas Strip is confirmed whole-event single-entry from an official source (checked 2026-11-17). All 37 Venetian DSE IV structure sheets, the Wynn daily structures, the WPT festival structures PDF, the Bellagio weekly PDFs and the Caesars flyer were read line by line. The only official entry-count statements are caps, not freezeouts: the WPT Main Event allows "a single entry per starting flight" (3 flights = up to 3 entries), WPT Prime allows one entry + one re-entry per flight, the Super Gold Million and most WPT side events price unlimited re-entry, and Venetian Events #18/#58 price "subsequent buy in's" — the rest is silence. Per policy, omission of re-entry language is not confirmation of single-entry.
