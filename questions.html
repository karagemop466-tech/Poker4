Questions and missing information

What the official record does not answer, what we have instead, and the exact route to close each one. Nothing here blocks the site from running; until an item is closed, the affected rows on the tournament, hotel and plan pages stay blank and flagged instead of being filled in.

Q1 · Which of these events actually forbid a second entry?

The whole brief is 'single entry only'. A $300 buy-in becomes $600, $900 or more if re-entries are unlimited, and the guarantee-to-cost comparison changes completely.

What we already have
All 37 official structure sheets for the series, read cover to cover on 2026-11-17: each one prints buy-in, house-fee split, guarantee, chips, level length and the late-registration deadline — and no clause about entries, re-entries, rebuys or add-ons. Two sheets (Valuetown bounty events 18/58) instead say 'All subsequent buy in's after the initial buy in will be $200', which positively indicates more than one entry is possible.
What is missing
A written answer from the Venetian Poker Room (or a rule update on the sheet) stating whether the one-day events are one-entry freezeouts.
How to close it
Ask the room directly (the poker page lists the contact) and keep the reply as a dated source; until then these rows are labelled 'single-entry unverified' and the toggle filter returns nothing.

Q2 · What are the exact trip dates, nights and occupancy, and what is the nightly budget and quality floor?

Hotel rates, cancellation deadlines and 'quality vs cost' are all date- and occupancy-dependent; without them there is no like-for-like comparison and no true nightly cost.

What we already have
Mandatory fees that do not depend on dates: $55 + tax resort fee at the Venetian/Palazzo, 13.38% room tax, $20 self-parking / $40 valet per day; $55 + tax at Wynn; $49.95 + tax at Treasure Island.
What is missing
A dated checkout total (nightly average, fee lines, tax lines) for each candidate hotel on your dates, plus your acceptable nightly ceiling and minimum quality tier.
How to close it
Price the same dates and occupancy in each official booking flow and paste the itemised totals into the rate log, or authorise a dated-quote step in a follow-up session; until then the calculator uses the advertised starting endpoints and labels them as such.

Q3 · Can a day really start at 8 AM here?

The brief asks for an all-day schedule beginning at 8 AM.

What we already have
Every printed start time on the official calendar: the earliest recurring morning start in the series is 10:10 AM (RaceDay Stack events 3–5, the $1,100 flights and the seniors events); one 9:10 AM turbo satellite exists on Dec 12.
What is missing
Any official 8:00 AM tournament start on the Strip in this window. None was found on any page checked, so an 8 AM start is not achievable from verified listings.
How to close it
Either relax the start-time requirement to 'first official flight' (10:10 AM at the Venetian) or name a property whose room opens earlier and have its schedule captured.

Q4 · When does each event actually end?

'Primary event + conditional fallback' only works if the earlier event can be finished or exited before the next registration deadline.

What we already have
Start time, the printed 'REG CLOSES' deadline for 80 of 93 rows, level count/length and the multi-flight Day-2 restart times from the official schedule and sheets.
What is missing
Announced finish times — the operator publishes none. Structures with 28 levels at 30 minutes plus six 15-minute breaks describe a 15-hour worst case, so a 5:10 PM entry after an 11:10 AM event is only realistic if you bust out before its own registration closes.
How to close it
Decide the exit rule (e.g. 'evening event only if bust-out before 4:30 PM') and the schedule page will flag each pair automatically; the underlying math is already wired.

Q5 · How long is the walk from each hotel to each poker room?

The 30-minute walking rule is a hard filter in the brief; it also decides whether an 8:00 AM-ish start and a late bust-out are survivable.

What we already have
Official property addresses and the official statement of where each poker room sits inside its resort (e.g. Venetian poker room on Palazzo level 2, reached from either tower lobby).
What is missing
Door-to-door walking minutes for every hotel↔venue pair. No property publishes them, and this build environment has no Maps access, so the table is blank by policy, not by oversight.
How to close it
Approve the maps-API step (S4) or supply the numbers you get from Google/Apple Maps; they land in data/walks.json and the ≤30-min filter switches from 'unverified' to enforced.

Q6 · Which guarantee figures are still safe to compare?

A guarantee that includes bounties, or one shared across several starting flights, is not the same object as a single-flight cash guarantee.

What we already have
Guarantee text for 69 of 93 printed rows, and the sheet-level split of buy-in → house fee vs prize-pool contribution for all 37 sheets. Bounty sheets say explicitly that the guarantee 'consists of the actual prize pool plus the total amount of $50 bounties'.
What is missing
Nothing blocking — but the ratio column must always be read as arithmetic on published numbers, not as an expectation.
How to close it
Keep bounty/flight/satellite rows in their own section (already implemented) and re-run the morning sync before any booking decision, since guarantees and start times are subject to change at the venue's discretion.

Q7 · What happens to the hotel rate if the poker-rate qualification is missed?

The headline poker rate is conditional; the true nightly cost can jump to the prevailing rate mid-stay.

What we already have
The official qualification terms: six hours of live cash per night, or a $300+ buy-in per night, completed before 2 AM on the last night; otherwise the nightly rate adjusts to the prevailing rate.
What is missing
The prevailing rate for your dates — which is the number the fallback actually uses.
How to close it
Get the dated prevailing rate alongside the poker rate in the same booking session and record both in the rate log; note that the $200 events on the calendar do not qualify for the poker rate at all.

Q9 · Does any Strip room actually forbid a second entry in writing, and will they put it in an e-mail?

It is the difference between the brief's single-entry list being empty and being populated. The published sheets for the Venetian series, the Wynn series and the Bellagio weekend events all permit re-entries; the Wynn daily Friday event is the only one that forbids re-entry while still selling unlimited rebuys and an add-on.

What we already have
Read and quoted, 2026-11-17: 37 Venetian structure sheets (0 state single-entry; 13 carry the 'bag for Day 2 then no further flight' rule; sheet 8 prints 'All subsequent buy in's after the initial buy in will be $200'); the Wynn WPT booklet (69 event sheets: 67 print 'unlimited re-entry', 2 print a one-entry-plus-one-re-entry cap, including the $10,400 World Championship's 'Four (4) Entries maximum'); the three Bellagio sheets ('buy-in and re-enter through the end of level 8'); and the Caesars page (silent).
What is missing
A room-written statement that a specific event is freezeout, plus which of those events the room will still be running on the trip dates.
How to close it
One e-mail or phone call per room (The Venetian 702.414.7657, Wynn 702.770.7654, Bellagio 702.693.7291) asking: for event X on date Y, is a player limited to one entry, and are rebuys or add-ons offered? Quote the answer and its date on the row.
