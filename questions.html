Suggested decision order

  1. Confirm trip dates and room occupancy.
  2. For each event, obtain explicit one-entry/no-re-entry/no-rebuy/no-add-on terms; exclude anything that does not qualify.
  3. Compare surviving tournaments on buy-in, house fee, guaranteed pool, blind structure, and schedule fit.
  4. Compare itemized hotel totals and verify the walk limit.
  5. Plan one event per day first; add an evening event only when no overlap is expected.
  6. Reconfirm official schedule and rates before nonrefundable payment.
