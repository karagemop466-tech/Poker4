Every sheet says TDA rules apply, which raises the question whether the rulebook itself forbids re-entry by default. TDA's rule on re-buys (No. 29 in the 2026 set) governs procedure when re-buys are offered - players may not miss a hand, chips bought are in play behind, etc. It does not state that a tournament without an announcement is a freezeout. Using it as proof would be inference, so the unverified labels stay.
