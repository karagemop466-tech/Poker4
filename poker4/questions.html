What must be answered before a decision is reasonable

Questions & missing information

Twelve questions, each with what is already known, what is missing, and the concrete action that closes it. Blocking questions are the ones where a wrong assumption costs money.

  1. For each candidate one-day event, does the room allow re-entry, rebuys or an add-on - and can it say so in writing?blocking

    We have: Every one-day sheet read so far states buy-in, fee split, late-registration close, payout percentage and chip counts, and stays silent on entry count. The Valuetown sheet prices 'subsequent buy-ins', which shows the room does spell it out when entries are repeatable.

    Missing: A yes/no per event number, in writing, for the events you would actually play.

    How to find out: Ask at the Venetian poker cage or by email for the shortlisted events; ask for the sentence to be added to the structure sheet. The monitor re-reads all 37 sheets, so a sheet that does state it will be caught.

    Who answers: Venetian Poker Room · related flag ↗

  2. Which exact dates, how many nights, and how many guests?blocking

    We have: Only the series window is verified: Nov 16 - Dec 23, 2026. The $55 + tax nightly resort fee was re-read today.

    Missing: Arrival/departure dates, guest count, room count. Without them there is no checkout total, no cancellation deadline and no like-for-like comparison.

    How to find out: Fix the window, then take one dated quote per hotel from each property's own booking engine and record the totals as dated rate-log rows.

    Who answers: The traveller · related flag ↗

  3. What is the per-night budget ceiling and the minimum acceptable quality standard?refines the plan

    We have: Nothing usable - no ceiling was supplied and no official quality metric exists in this data set.

    Missing: A number and a standard (for example 'not above $X all-in per night').

    How to find out: State both and the hotel table ranks instead of merely listing. If quality must stay on official sources, agree which official attribute counts (brand, tower, suite class).

    Who answers: The traveller · related flag ↗

  4. Which walking routes are actually within 30 minutes of the Venetian poker room, door to door?blocking

    We have: Official pages put the poker room on Palazzo Level 2 with directions from both resort lobbies; a same-property stay needs no street route.

    Missing: Minutes for every candidate hotel, with the source and date of that measurement.

    How to find out: Open the route links on the Hotels page, read each walking time and store it as a dated observation; the <=30 min filter only means something after that.

    Who answers: A live map service, recorded as an observation · related flag ↗

  5. When does each event really end - or how late can you leave one and still make the next?refines the plan

    We have: Start times and late-registration closes are stated ($300 one-day: 11:10 AM start, closes ~4:55 PM; RaceDay: 10:10 AM, closes ~12:25 PM). Multi-flight Day 1 ends at about 12.5% of the field remaining, with no clock time.

    Missing: Expected finish, or a scheduled Day 1 end, for the events you want to stack.

    How to find out: Ask the room for typical Day 1 finishes by field size. Until then the plan shows one primary event plus a conditional fallback and never promises both.

    Who answers: Venetian Poker Room · related flag ↗

  6. Is there a real 8 AM start anywhere on the Strip in this window?refines the plan

    We have: On the official Venetian calendar the earliest start of any row is 9:10 AM (a Dec 12 satellite); the earliest main-event start is 10:00-10:10 AM. No 8 AM start exists here.

    Missing: Any 8 AM start at any room, or acceptance that the day begins at 10:10 AM.

    How to find out: Have the monitor report each room's earliest start daily; if none exists, rebuild the day plan around the real earliest start.

    Who answers: Each poker room · related flag ↗

    We have: Events #3, #4, #5 and #6 show no guarantee line on the calendar or their sheets.

    Missing: Guarantee yes/no and amount.

    How to find out: Ask the room. Then those four low-cost events can enter the value comparison instead of being excluded from it.

    Who answers: Venetian Poker Room · related flag ↗
