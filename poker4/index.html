All 12 questions with the answers we have

Plan constraints found, not assumed

No 8 AM start exists here

Earliest row on the official calendar is 9:10 AM (a Dec 12 satellite); earliest main-event start is 10:00–10:10 AM. The day plan is built from real starts, not a wish.

Finish times are not published

Sheets give start and late-registration close only, so overlaps are flagged as "cannot register for both" or "conditional on busting out in time" — never as a certainty.

Hotel total needs your dates

$55 + tax nightly fee is confirmed; the room rate is not a single stored number but a dated log, so no nightly total is claimed for undated stays.

Where the money goes per entry

The official sheets state the house fee is withheld from the advertised buy-in, not added on top: e.g. "$58 of the $300 Buy-In is withheld for house fees … the prize pool consists of $242 of every $300 Buy In." That is the number to compare across buy-in levels.
