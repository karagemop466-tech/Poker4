Estimated trip cost = entries × buy-ins + nights × true hotel cost, where true hotel cost/night = (nightly rate + mandatory resort fee) × 1.1338 tax. Parking is shown as a separate optional line. Defaults come from the verified dated log where one exists; every input is editable. Numbers you type are not saved or sent anywhere.

What the output means

  • Entries × buy-ins — total tournament cost at one entry per event (rebuys/add-ons would change this; those events are in separate sections).
  • True hotel cost/night — (rate + resort fee) × (1 + tax). This is the mandatory nightly cost; parking is optional and listed separately.
  • Checkout total — nights × (rate + fee) plus tax on both, matching how the Venetian terms bill (“Daily rates do not include the applicable Clark County Room Tax of 13.38%”, resort fee “$55 plus tax per night”).
  • Nightly average — checkout total ÷ nights, for like-for-like comparisons.
