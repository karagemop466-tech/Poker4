Estimated cost of a trip

Trip cost calculator

Estimated trip cost = entries × buy-ins + nights × true hotel cost per night. Every buy-in below comes from the official Venetian DeepStack Extravaganza IV schedule and structure sheets (schedule re-verified 17 Nov 2026). The hotel portion uses only officially sourced fee and tax values; a date-specific room rate must be entered by you from an official booking quote — none is invented here.

How the hotel math works (official components only). True cost per night = (room rate + $55 resort fee) × 1.1338 (Clark County room tax) + parking if needed. Sources: resort fee $55 + tax ↗ · 13.38% room tax, $20/day guest parking, $40 valet ↗ · poker suite rates from $119/$209 (conditional) ↗. All re-read 17 Nov 2026.

3 · Estimate

This is an estimate, not a quote. Preset rates ($119/$209) are advertised starting endpoints subject to qualifying poker play (6 hours of cash or a $300+ buy-in per night — official terms ↗), availability and blackouts; they are not prices for any specific night. A non-qualifying stay reprices to the prevailing rate. Enter your own official booking quote for a real figure.
Formula: Σ(selected buy-ins) + nights × ((rate + $55) × 1.1338 + parking). Buy-in data: official DSE IV schedule ↗ (re-verified 17 Nov 2026). Not included: flights, food, tips, cash-game buy-ins, or any cost not listed above.
