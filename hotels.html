How to read “true nightly cost”

Confirmed mandatory fee

$55 resort fee per night before tax. The property terms specify a 13.38% Clark County room tax and say the resort fee is subject to applicable tax. Arithmetic: $55 × 1.1338 = $62.359, rounded to $62.36/night.

Official hotel terms ↗

Illustrative lowest endpoint

The rate page advertises poker rates starting as low as $119 and $209 (not tied to a specific property in the copy). If a $119 base rate is available for a date, standard double occupancy, before extras: ($119 + $55) × 1.1338 = $197.28/night. At $209, the same calculation is $299.32/night.

Official poker-rate terms ↗

Qualification matters

To keep the poker rate, one guest must play six hours of active live cash each night or buy in to a $300+ tournament each night, before 2 AM on the last night. If not met, the nightly rate adjusts to prevailing rate. A $200 event alone does not satisfy the stated tournament threshold.

Official play requirements ↗

Do not treat the figures above as the vacation bill. They omit the actual room quote, possible date-specific pricing, taxes/charges beyond the stated standard room tax and resort fee, optional upgrades/additional guests, and any offer failure/prevailing-rate adjustment. Current trip dates and guest count are necessary to get an exact total. Official terms also say rates vary with length of stay and availability.
