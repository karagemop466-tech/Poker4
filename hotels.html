Reading a blank honestly

Where a property shows rates only inside its booking calendar, no number is copied onto this site — the booking link is given instead and the row is marked. Two official pages currently return 404 (Bellagio's resort-fee page and the Caesars Palace daily flyer); they are reported as unreachable, never replaced by a third-party mirror. ARIA publishes two different resort fees on two official pages ($50 and $55): both sentences are shown and neither is chosen for you. No official walking-minute figure exists for any hotel→poker-room pair on the Strip, so the ≤30-minute rule is carried as a warning, not a green tick — F6 explains how to close it.

No value on this site is estimated. A number appears only because the venue or hotel printed it on its own page or PDF; the link and the date it was read sit next to it. A blank cell means the official source did not publish that value, and a flag says what would close the gap.
