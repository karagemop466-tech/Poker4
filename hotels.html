Hotels & true cost

True cost per night = (nightly rate + mandatory resort fee) × (1 + 13.38% Clark County room tax). Parking is added per night in the calculator. Rates shown are official published leads with timestamps — not dated booking quotes. For a like-for-like comparison, price the same dates and room type on each official booking link and record it in the rate log (checkout total, nightly average, cancellation deadline, timestamp).

Comparison rules (like-for-like)

  • Same dates, same occupancy, same room class (standard king/queen), tax and mandatory fee included.
  • Record checkout total and nightly average, the resort fee and tax rate used, parking, the cancellation deadline, and the exact timestamp of the quote.
  • Conditional poker rates (Venetian $119/$209) require qualifying play — count the buy-in/hours as part of the cost when comparing.
  • Parking: Venetian guests $20/day; MGM properties $20/day guests ($20–25 non-guests); Wynn and TI include parking.
