GENERAL RULES

01. Players start with 40,000 in tournament chips (20,000 + 20,000 optional).

02. Levels will be 30 minutes in length with a 15-minute break every three (3) levels. (Day 1D will have 25-minute levels)

03. Registration will be open until the start of Level 10. (Approximately 5:15pm for Flights 1A, 1B, 4:15pm for 1C and 9:30pm for Flight D).

04. Combined Final Day 2 (9/13/26) will begin at 12pm at Bellagio. Final Day levels 40 minutes, final three (3) players’ levels will be 30
    minutes. Tournament Redraws will occur at the completion of Day 1 for a combined Day 2 at Bellagio.

05. Tournament will be played 9-handed on Day 1. Starting Day 2, all tables will be 8-handed. Final table will be 9-handed.

06. Each Flight would play until the money (12.5%). Must bag chips to make Day 2 and be in the money.

07. Of the $600 Buy-in, $510 goes to the Prize Pool, $50 is Admin Fees and $40 is optional staff gratuity.

08. Bellagio guarantees that the total Combined Prize Pool will be at least $50,000.

09. Bellagio Poker does not withhold any percentage of the prize pool for staff gratuities. There is a voluntary staff gratuity of $40, at
    registration only. The voluntary staff gratuity will grant players an extra 20,000 chips. This optional gratuity is paid to staff and will not
    be kept by the house.

10. Ante is posted from the big blind only and big blind is posted first.

11. Tournament tickets are non-refundable and non-transferrable. Pre-purchased seats will have their chips in play from the beginning of
    the tournament.

12. Winners will be paid in casino chips. Residents of foreign countries without a U. S. tax treaty will be subject to withholding.

13. Management reserves the right to change or cancel tournaments.
