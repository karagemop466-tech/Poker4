For Registered Hotel Guests

Your 24-hour parking fee provides you with in-out privileges within the same parking type (self or valet) across all MGM Resorts Las Vegas destinations within the same 24 hour period (subject to availability). Use your room key to access all parking facilities until 11:59 p.m. on the day of check out.
