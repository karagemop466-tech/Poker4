 From The Venetian Lobby:</b></p>
<p>Proceed across The Venetian casino floor, to Restaurant Row. Enter the Waterfall Atrium. Look for the escalator by the Waterfall Atrium. Take the escalator up to level 2, Grand Canal Shoppes. Make a u-turn and take the hallway to the right. Turn right at EJ Pretty and The Poker Room will be on your right.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><b>From The Palazzo Lobby:</b></p>
<p>Proceed across The Palazzo Casino Floor to the self-parking elevators. Take the elevator up to level 2, Grand Canal Shoppes. Enter Grand Canal Shoppes and take a left at Liam’s Den and Bubble Bar. The Poker Room will be straight ahead.</p>
