1. These General Poker Tournament Terms and Conditions are meant to be read and followed in conjunction 
with Wynn’s standard poker rules, and each individual Tournament event’s (may be referred to as “Event” or 
collectively as a “Tournament”) Terms and Conditions and Structure Sheet. In the event there is a conflict, 
these Terms and Conditions shall prevail. 
2. Players and guests must be at least 21 years of age. 
3. Registration for each Event will open four (4) hours prior to the scheduled start of play. Players will register 
in the Wynn Las Vegas Poker Room, located in Encore at Wynn Las Vegas. 
4. The Tournament entry fee (the “Entry Fee”) must be paid with cash or Wynn Las Vegas live casino chips; no 
other forms of payment will be accepted. 
5. All Events will use Tournament chips for play. Tournament chips have no cash value, may not be used for 
any other purpose, and must be returned to Wynn Las Vegas at the end of each Event.   
6. All Events will utilize the Big Blind Ante format unless otherwise noted on the Tournament structure sheet. In 
the event that the player in the big blind has less than the required amount to post both the big blind and the 
ante, the big blind will be posted first. The ante will then be posted with any remaining Tournament chips.  
7. A redraw for seats will occur at the conclusion of each day for all multi-flight and multi-day Events, as well as 
when each tournament reaches the final table. All Events will redraw when the Event reaches the final table.  
8. When the tournament reaches the final five (5) tables, the next table to “break” will be selected by random 
draw by the Tournament Director. This process will continue for each table thereafter until the final table 
redraw is reached.   
9. Tournament chip stacks will be put into play at the start of the event for all registered players who have been 
issued a table and seat assignment. Players registered as an “alternate” will receive a seat assignment from 
the Tournament Staff when seating becomes available.  
10. At the conclusion of the registration period, all paid entries (including alternates) who have yet to be seated 
will be assigned a seat and their Tournament chips will be put into play. 
11. A player will forfeit their option to cancel (void) their entry once their Tournament chip stack has been put 
into play and will not be entitled to a refund. 
12. Timely play is expected of all players, and stalling is not permitted. Intentional stalling in an effort to gain an 
advantage over the field is strictly prohibited and may result in a penalty, including during events that feature 
an Action Clock. The use of time extensions in unwarranted situations, specifically when players are 
approaching a pay jump, will be considered a stalling violation and may result in the forfeiture of all 
remaining time extension chips. 
13. At the sole discretion of the Tournament Director, a single hand taking considerably longer to complete than 
the rest of the field, during which time one or more players at other tables are eliminated, resulting in a pay 
jump, will result in prize money being split evenly between all players eliminated during such time. 
14. Winners will be paid in Wynn Las Vegas live casino chips.

scheduled dates of play, start times, and other relevant structure information.

Tournament Action Clock 
General Terms and Conditions 
 
 
1. Dealers will initiate the start of the Tournament Action Clock upon the completion of the deal. Time will 
begin for a player once the natural flow of play has come to a brief pause pending their action and be 
reset to the corresponding time for the next player to act. 
2. Players will have fifteen (15) seconds on their Action Clock preflop, unless otherwise specified on the 
official tournament structure sheet.  
3. Once a player has voluntarily placed chips into the pot, the Tournament Action Clock will increase to 
thirty (30) seconds for each subsequent action of that player until the hand has concluded.  
4. The small and big blind are forced bets and are not considered voluntary.  
5. When a bet or raise has been made, the dealer will count and announce the bet or raise (including All 
In bets) prior to starting the Action Clock for the next player.  
6. To facilitate the smooth operation of the tournament, all tournament chips must remain in full stacks of 
twenty (20) chips, when available.  
7. When breaking a table or moving a player to another table, a player must keep their time extensions 
and tournament chips in plain view while traveling to their new seat.  
8. Each time extension will add an additional thirty (30) seconds to act. 
9. Players will receive time extension chips at predetermined stages (set level or remaining number of 
players) as outlined below for each specific event.  
10. Players are required to keep all time extension chips visible and on the table at all times.  
11. Players will automatically lose one (1) time extension each time the Action Clock reaches zero (0), and 
will be instructed by the dealer to turn in the corresponding amount of time extensions used at the 
completion of their action. Players are not responsible for placing a time extension chip into the pot in 
real time.  
12. In the event that a player does not have any time extensions remaining and the Action Clock reached 
zero (0), the player’s hand will be declared dead (or the player will be forced to check if facing no 
action). 
13. Once implemented, the Tournament Action Clock will be used until the event has reached the final 
table. 
14. At the conclusion of play each day, any unused time extension chips will be placed in the players’ 
tournament chip bag and will carry over to the next tournament day. Time extensions are to be treated 
with the same level of importance as tournament chips, and removal of time extensions may be subject 
to penalty up to and including disqualification. 
15. See individual event rules for additional details.
