1. TDA rules apply to all Venetian Poker Room tournaments. 
2. Open to Female players who must 21+ years of age and have a Venetian Rewards 
card to participate. 
3. Management reserves the right to cancel or change the tournament.  
4. Winners will be awarded direct entry to Event #39 and any odd money payouts may 
choose to be paid in casino chips or cash.  
5. Winners must show a valid ID to collect winnings. Residents of foreign countries 
without a U.S. tax treaty will be subject to withholding. Pursuant to IRS regulations, 
W-2G or 1042 forms will be issued for all tournament winnings of $5,000 or more 
(net of buy-in amount).  
6. $20 of the $100 Buy-In is withheld for house fees (Rake + Staff). The prize pool 
consists of $80 of every $100 Buy In.  
7. Ante will come from the Big Blind Only. Big Blind is posted before the Ante.  
8. All tournament prizes are direct entry into our $800 No Limit Hold’em LIPS Ladies 
Event #39, with any odd monies awarded as one additional payout.   
9. A Player who accumulates 200,000 in tournament chips (“The Milestone”) or more 
will instantly receive direct entry into the $800 No Limit Hold’em LIPS ladies Event 
#39. Upon verification by poker room management, the player who achieved The 
Milestone will cease play and have their chips removed from play.  
10. The tournament will conclude when the number of players remaining is equal to the 
remaining number of $800 No Limit Hold’em LIPS Ladies Event #39 direct entry 
seats to be awarded, less the number of Milestone $800 No Limit Hold’em LIPS 
Ladies Event #39 direct entry seats already awarded previously. Any odd monies 
will be awarded to the last player eliminated prior to the conclusion of the 
tournament. It is the players’ responsibility to identify when they have reached the 
Milestone, and to notify poker room management accordingly. Any player dealt into 
a hand must complete the hand before verification of the Milestone by poker room 
management. Any player who subsequently falls below the Milestone, before 
verification can be completed, must once again reach the Milestone and have their 
chips verified, in order to be eligible for the $800 No Limit Hold’em LIPS Ladies 
Event #39 direct entry seat prize.  
11. Only a member of Poker room management can verify official chip counts. 
However, any other player at the table or the dealer can help identify and alert any 
other player who may have reached the Milestone. To better assist with 
identification of Milestones, and to assist with the counting process, players must 
keep their chips in stacks of twenty (20) chips.  
12. All pre-registered stacks will be in play at the start of the event.        
13. The non seat prize pool cash disbursement, as set forth herein, may be modified 
upon unanimous written agreement of management and of all tournament players 
still involved in the tournament.
