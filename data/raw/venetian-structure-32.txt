1. TDA rules apply to all Venetian Poker Room tournaments. 
2. Must be a female 21+ years of age and have a Venetian Rewards card to participate. 
3. Day 1 will play until we are down to approximately 12.5% of the field remaining. 
Day 2 will resume at 10:00 AM with all remaining players being considered in the 
money & will continue until seven (7) players remain for a produced and distributed 
streamed final table by PokerGO. The final table will begin at approximately 7:00 
PM. To begin Day 2, the clock will resume at the stoppage time of Day 1. 
4. Management reserves the right to cancel or change the tournament.  
5. Winners may choose to be paid in casino chips or cash.  
6. Winners must show a valid ID to collect winnings. Residents of foreign countries 
without a U.S. tax treaty will be subject to withholding. Pursuant to IRS regulations, 
W-2G or 1042 forms will be issued for all tournament winnings of $5,000 or more 
(net of buy-in amount).  
7. $90 of the $800 Buy-In is withheld for house fees (Rake + Staff). $20 of the $800 
Buy In will be withheld as a flat fee for Vegas Connected, INC, DBA Ladies 
International Poker Series (LIPS). Prize pool consists of $690 of every $800 Buy In.  
8. Ante will come from the Big Blind Only. Big Blind is posted before the Ante.  
9. All pre-registered stacks will be in play at the start of the event.        
10. The tournament prize pool disbursement, as set forth herein, may be modified upon 
unanimous written agreement of management and of all tournament players still 
involved in the tournament.  
11. On Day 2, at Tournament Directors Discretion, players will have twenty (20) 
seconds pre-flop and thirty (30) seconds post-flop to act on their hand through the 
use of a countdown clock. Based on Day 2 field size, players will receive the 
following time-extension chips, each worth a 30-second addition to a player’s 
action: 
a. Less than 46 players: 5-time extension chips.  
b. 46-72 players: 3-time extension chips + 3 at 27 players remaining. 
c. 73-99 players: 4-time extension chips + 3 at 27 players remaining. 
d. 100 + players: 5-time extension chips + 3 at 27 players remaining 
12. In the case that time expires for a player with remaining time-extension chips, the 
player’s countdown clock will automatically extend an additional thirty (30) 
seconds. Collection of time-extension chips will take place at the end of the player’s 
action. If a player’s countdown clock expires and the player does not have any time-
extension chips remaining, they will be (a) forced to check if there is no bet pending 
or (b) fold, if facing a bet or raise. Shot Clocks will be removed at the Final Table.
