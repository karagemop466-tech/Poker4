Wynn Daily Poker Tournaments 
General Terms and Conditions 
 
1. These General Poker Tournament Terms and Conditions are meant to be read and followed in 
conjunction with Wynn’s standard poker tournament rules, and each individual Tournament event’s 
(may be referred to as “Event” or collectively as a “Tournament”) Terms and Conditions and Structure 
Sheet. In the event there is a conflict, these Terms and Conditions shall prevail. 
 
2. Players and guests must be at least 21 years of age. 
 
3. Registration for each Event will open four (4) hours prior to the scheduled start of play. Players will 
register in the Wynn Las Vegas Poker Room, located in Encore at Wynn Las Vegas. 
 
4. The Tournament entry fee (the “Entry Fee”) must be paid with cash or Wynn Las Vegas live casino 
chips; no other forms of payment will be accepted. 
 
5. All Events will use Tournament chips for play. Tournament chips have no cash value, may not be used 
for any other purpose, and must be returned to Wynn Las Vegas at the end of each Event.   
 
6. All Events will utilize the Big Blind Ante format unless otherwise noted on the Tournament structure 
sheet.  In the event that the player in the big blind has less than the required amount to post both the 
big blind and the ante, the big blind will be posted first. The ante will then be posted with any remaining 
Tournament chips.  
 
7. Tournament chip stacks will be put into play at the start of the event for all registered players who have 
been issued a table and seat assignment. Players registered as an “alternate” will receive a seat 
assignment from the Tournament Staff when seating becomes available. In events where applicable, 
the tournament chip stack of players who have received a discount for early registration will be put into 
play at the start of the event. 
 
8. At the conclusion of the registration period, all paid entries (including alternates) who have yet to be 
seated will be assigned a seat and their Tournament chips will be put into play. 
 
9. A player will forfeit their option to cancel (void) their entry once their Tournament chip stack has been 
put into play and will not be entitled to a refund.   
 
10. Winners will be paid in Wynn Las Vegas live casino chips. 
 
11. Players will need to refer to each Event’s structure sheet for applicable details including but not limited 
to the scheduled dates of play, start times, and other relevant structure information. 
 
12. IRS Compliance requires each player provide a valid government issued ID and a social security 
number. Nonresident aliens are subject to 30% US Income Tax withholding. ITIN's are required for 
Treaty Country exemptions. Tax form W-2G will be issued to all players winning in excess of $5,000, 
net of the applicable Tournament Entry Fee. 
 
13. Wynn Las Vegas standard poker tournament rules (found at wynnpoker.com and inside the Wynn 
Poker Room) and current standard TDA rules (found at https://www.pokertda.com/poker-tda-rules/) 
apply. 
 
14. While players are required to be a member of the Wynn Rewards program in order to participate in a 
Tournament, Wynn Rewards has its own separate and distinct terms and conditions. Please visit the 
Wynn Rewards desk for details. 
 
15. Players and guests will be required to adhere to all applicable Wynn Las Vegas policies. Wynn Resorts 
requires appropriate and respectful interactions between our guests and employees in our 
resorts. Wynn Resorts reserves the right to trespass anyone exhibiting behavior, language or clothing 
that, at our discretion, is inconsistent with the Wynn Resorts behavior standards. The Wynn behavior 
standards and other applicable policies can be accessed at www.wynnlasvegas.com. In the event a 
player or their guests are prevented from accessing property as a result of such policies or trespassed 
from property for non-compliance with such policies, then player shall be prohibited from participating in 
the Event or Tournament series and will not be entitled to any prizes or gifts associated with the Event 
or Tournament series. 
 
16. Wynn Las Vegas reserves the right to refuse entry to, or disqualify, any individual at any time before or 
during the Event or Tournament series for any reason in Wynn Las Vegas’ sole discretion. 
 
17. Players must be clearly identifiable at all times. Tournament staff may request a player to remove any 
material (sunglasses, hat, hood, or other facial cover) which inhibits their continuous identification or is 
a distraction to the other participants. Players are not allowed to wear sunglasses and a face cover at 
the same time. 
 
18. Players who are unable to participate or are disqualified from an Event are not entitled to any refunds or 
payouts. 
 
19. Wynn Las Vegas management reserves the right to revise, suspend, or cancel events at its sole 
discretion, without prior notice or liability. 
 
20. Prior to the start of the Tournament, players may be required to execute a Film, Videotape, and/or 
Photograph Release as a condition of participating in the Tournament.  If a player fails to execute a 
Film, Videotape, and/or Photograph Release, Wynn reserves the right to refuse entry to such individual.  
 
 
21. Participants are prohibited from using gaming charts or any other poker training tools in the 
Tournament Area while play is in progress.  The “Tournament Area” is defined as the poker tables on 
which play is underway, as well as any adjacent walkways or spaces that are accessible to participants 
and in view of any table on which play is taking place.  Any participant found to be using gaming charts 
or any other poker training tools will be penalized in Wynn Las Vegas’ sole discretion, up to and 
including disqualification. 
 
22. The use of gaming charts or other poker training tools by spectators, family, friends, or coaches of the 
participants in or around the Tournament Area for the purpose of relaying real time information, 
strategy, or coaching advice is expressly prohibited during tournament play.   Additionally, participants 
may not receive strategy advice or coaching derived from charts or training tools (i.e., what hands 
should and should not be played in any given circumstance). Wynn Las Vegas reserves the right to limit 
discussion and/or remove spectators from the area, as well as issue penalties to participants up to and 
including disqualification for any violation, at its sole discretion.   
 
23. Scheduled breaks, as outlined by the tournament structure, fall outside of tournament play. 
 
24. At the Tournament Director’s discretion, Wynn Las Vegas reserves the right to limit the use of 
electronic devices including, but not limited to cellular phones, headphones, earbuds, or wearable 
technology (e.g., smartwatches) at any time.   
 
25. Eyewear that features an electronic component, which includes wireless or Bluetooth capabilities, are 
expressly prohibited.  Any participant found wearing such eyewear, regardless of whether the electronic 
components and/or features are in use or powered on, will be penalized in Wynn Las Vegas’ sole 
discretion, up to and including disqualification. 
 
26. All electronic devices, including those outlined above as well as those not expressly defined, will not be 
permitted during final table play and must be stowed and not accessed by the participant.  Electronic 
devices may be used outside of the Tournament Area, provided they are not being used for the 
purpose of violating the previous stipulations outlined above. 
 
27. During final table play and all other timeframes during which the Tournament Director has limited or 
restricted the use of electronic devices, hooded sweatshirts and other apparel may not be worn in a 
manner that covers a participant’s ears. 
 
 
28. Filming or streaming live action or participant’s hole cards by participants and spectators is prohibited 
without prior authorization from Wynn Las Vegas Management. Any participant found to be in violation 
may be penalized in Wynn Las Vegas’ sole discretion, up to and including disqualification. 
 
29. The Tournament series and these Terms and Conditions are subject to Nevada Revised Statutes 
463.362-463.3668 and Nevada Gaming Commission Regulation 7A and, subject thereto, all decisions 
made by Wynn Las Vegas shall be deemed final. 
 
30. Problem Gambling HelpLine 1-(800)-522-4700. Know Your Limits®.
