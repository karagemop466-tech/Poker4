1. TDA rules apply to all Venetian Poker Room tournaments. 
2. Must be 21+ years of age and have a Venetian Rewards card to participate. 
3. Each starting flight will play until we are down to approximately 12.5% of 
the field remaining. Day 2 will resume at 12:00 PM combining all 
remaining players from 1A, 1B, 1C, and 1D with all players being 
considered in the money & will continue until a winner has been declared. 
To begin Day 2, the clock will revert to the earliest stoppage time of Day 1. 
4. Management reserves the right to cancel or change the tournament.  
5. Winners may choose to be paid in casino chips or cash.  
6. Winners must show a valid ID to collect winnings. Residents of foreign 
countries without a U.S. tax treaty will be subject to withholding. Pursuant 
to IRS regulations, W-2G or 1042 forms will be issued for all tournament 
winnings of $5,000 or more (net of buy-in amount).  
7. $60 of the $300 Buy-In is withheld for house fees (Rake + Staff). The prize 
pool consists of $240 of every $300 Buy In.  
8. Any player who bags chips for Day 2 may not play a subsequent flight.  
9. Ante will come from the Big Blind Only. Big Blind is posted before Ante.  
10. All pre-registered stacks will be in play at the start of the event.        
11. The tournament prize pool disbursement, as set forth herein, may be 
modified upon unanimous written agreement of management and of all 
tournament players still involved in the tournament. 
12. On Day 2, at Tournament Directors Discretion, players will have twenty 
(20) seconds pre-flop and thirty (30) seconds post-flop to act on their hand 
through the use of a countdown clock. Based on Day 2 field size, players 
will receive the following time-extension chips, each worth a 30-second 
addition to a player’s action: 
a. Less than 46 players: 5-time extension chips.  
b. 46-72 players: 3-time extension chips + 3 at 27 players remaining. 
c. 73-99 players: 4-time extension chips + 3 at 27 players remaining. 
d. 100 + players: 5-time extension chips + 3 at 27 players remaining. 
13. In the case that time expires for a player with remaining time-extension 
chips, the player’s countdown clock will automatically extend an additional 
thirty (30) seconds. Collection of time-extension chips will take place at the 
end of the player’s action. If a player’s countdown clock expires and the 
player does not have any time-extension chips remaining, they will be (a) 
forced to check if there is no bet pending or (b) fold, if facing a bet or raise. 
Shot Clocks will be removed at the Final Table.
