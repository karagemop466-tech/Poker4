# Hotel Data — Walking Distance to Strip Poker Tournaments (research as of 2026-11-16)

Goal: best hotels within walking distance (≤ 30 minutes / ~1.5 miles) of the Strip tournament venues (Venetian, Wynn/Encore, ARIA, Horseshoe, Mandalay Bay, Planet Hollywood).
Cost and quality are higher priority than distance; distance must still be reasonable (≤ 30 min walk).
True cost per night = base room rate + mandatory resort fee + Clark County lodging tax (13.38% on the Strip, applied to room + fee) + self-parking (if driving).
Since May 2025, advertised hotel prices on the Strip must include the resort fee (FTC rule); tax and parking are added at checkout.
Specific Nov/Dec 2026 base room rates were NOT confirmed from official booking engines for specific dates — typical ranges below are from published guides and are flagged unverified. Resort fees and parking fees are verified where a source is listed.

Walking times are approximate, computed from resort addresses along the Las Vegas Strip at ~3 mph (1 mile ≈ 20 min); they are estimates, not quoted from a source.

## Money-saving notes (verified where sourced)
- MGM Rewards Gold tier and above: resort fee waived on up to two rooms per night at ARIA, Bellagio, Cosmopolitan, Mandalay Bay, Park MGM, MGM Grand, Wynn/Encore, and others (booked direct, in your name) — H9.
- Caesars Rewards Platinum and above: free self-parking at Caesars Strip properties (Venetian/Palazzo, Caesars, Planet Hollywood, etc.) — H2, H9.
- Circa Las Vegas has NO poker room (confirmed via circalasvegas.com, H10) — not a poker base, though it is downtown (not walking distance to Strip venues anyway).

## Walking-distance sanity check by venue cluster
- Wynn ↔ Venetian: ~1.0 mile ≈ 20 min walk — reasonable.
- Venetian ↔ ARIA: ~0.7 mile ≈ 15 min walk — reasonable.
- ARIA ↔ Mandalay Bay: ~1.1 miles ≈ 22 min walk — reasonable.
- Wynn ↔ Mandalay Bay: ~2.8 miles ≈ 55 min walk — NOT reasonable; do not plan to walk between these two.
- All day-play (8 AM start): no tournament in the research window starts at 8 AM. Earliest confirmed starts are 10:10 AM (Venetian DSE) and 11:00 AM (Wynn/Horseshoe).
