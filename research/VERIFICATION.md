## Executive finding

**No upcoming tournament reviewed is confirmed by its current official rules as a strict single-entry event.** This is a statement about the reviewed evidence, not proof that no such tournament exists. An event with no published entry cap is **unknown**, not single-entry. A one-entry-per-flight event is also not single-entry overall when a player may enter other flights or another Day 2 entry.

The closest official candidates are Venetian DeepStack one-day events: their schedules, buy-ins, start times, guarantees (where published), and fee splits are documented, but the reviewed one-day structure sheets do not say whether re-entry, rebuy, or add-on is prohibited. They therefore stay out of the qualifying list. Caesars Palace, Wynn, MGM Grand, and Bellagio have currently published formats with re-entry, add-ons, rebuys, or optional extra chips and are excluded. Horseshoe, ARIA, and Westgate publish some daily-tournament information but not enough event terms to qualify anything.

No reviewed official schedule lists an 8:00 a.m. tournament start. The earliest recurring published start reviewed is 9:00 a.m. at Horseshoe, but its event buy-ins and entry rules are not published in the reviewed official sources. In the specifically dated Venetian candidates, Event #1 starts Nov. 17 at 11:10 a.m.; the later $200 RaceDay Stack dates start at 10:10 a.m. Nov. 19–21. The latter’s entry terms remain unverified.

## Qualification method

A tournament qualifies only if official event-specific terms establish all of the following:

1. **One total paid entry per person for the event**, not one per starting flight.
2. No re-entry, rebuy, add-on, or other paid route to extra chips or an additional live entry.
3. The date, venue, start time, and buy-in are published for the relevant upcoming event.

Optional staff gratuities that buy more chips are flagged as spend-based advantages. Bounty formats alone are not treated as additional-entry opportunities, but they still need an explicit entry cap. Missing terms are never interpreted as “no re-entry.”
