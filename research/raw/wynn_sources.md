One (1) $100 add-on for 15,000 tournament chips is available any time until the end of registration." → **Unlimited rebuys + 1 add-on CONFIRMED; re-entry explicitly NOT allowed.**
3. **$200 NLH Sat & Sun, $25,000 GTD** — Single Day, begins 12 p.m. — $200 = $26 + $12 + $162; "$5 from each $100 add-on withheld as Tournament fee and $5 ... as service charge" — "Registration, re-entries, and one (1) add-on per entry will be available until the start of level 11 (approximately 5:30 p.m.)" — 25,000 chips — "One (1) $100 add-on for 25,000 tournament chips is available any time until the end of registration." → **Re-entry + 1 add-on CONFIRMED.**
4. **$160 NLH Nightly Mon–Sun, $10,000 GTD** — Single Day, begins 6 p.m. — $160 = $20 + $10 + $130 — "Registration, re-entries, and one (1) add-on per entry will be available until the start of level 9 (approximately 9:00 p.m.)" — 25,000 chips — 20-min levels, 10-min breaks. → **Re-entry + 1 add-on CONFIRMED.**

## Findings
- Wynn poker room is in ENCORE (printed in general terms).
- Every Wynn daily event officially allows re-entry and/or add-ons EXCEPT the Friday $240 Rebuy & Add-on (rebuys/add-on yes, re-entry explicitly no).
- Every WPT-series event whose structure was read officially allows unlimited re-entry.
- Therefore: ZERO Wynn events qualify as confirmed single-entry. Wynn rows go in the re-entry/add-on section with official excerpts.
