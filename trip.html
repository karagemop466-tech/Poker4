Formula and honesty rules
  • Total = (entries × buy-in) + (nights × true nightly hotel cost), where true nightly cost = rate + mandatory resort fee + 13.38% tax on (rate + fee).
  • The hotel term uses the latest dated capture when one exists (shown with its capture date and stay window), otherwise the conditional poker rate when verified (marked "approx."). No rate is ever invented; if none exists the calculator refuses to total and says so.
  • Not included: airfare, food, ground transport, add-ons ($50 Caesars add-on etc.), re-entry beyond the entries you declare, and incidentals holds ($150/night at Wynn/Venetian — a hold, not a charge).
