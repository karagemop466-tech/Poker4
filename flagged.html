  • F16 Walking times are measured distances at an assumed pace, not official minutes

    method: OpenStreetMap foot-route length between each property's building point and each event room's building point, converted to minutes at a stated pace; pace: 80.0 m per minute; why not the server durations: the demo router's foot durations came back at 4-8 m/s, which is not walking speed, so only its route lengths were used; cross-checks: 12 pairs were re-routed one at a time and agreed to within 0.05 m; captures: data/raw/f0d7de66-geocode.txt, data/raw/f0d7de66-osrm.txt. No Strip property publishes a door-to-door walking time to another property's poker room, so these are the closest thing to a measured figure that exists, and every cell carries official:false. The points are building centroids, so an interior connection (Venetian-Palazzo, Wynn-Encore) is not represented by the street route.
