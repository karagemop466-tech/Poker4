What “survived” means here, and what it does not

An event is on this page because the fee split and the guarantee are both printed by the official document, so the ratio beside it can be recomputed, and because it is an entry rather than a Day-2 continuation. Every row still carries its own entry rules.

No event in this snapshot is confirmed single-entry. That gate is not applied here because it removes nothing — 0 of 253 rows carry a single-entry confirmation, and the reason is recorded on flag F1 and in the source text panel of every row on the tournaments page. A sheet that prints nothing about entries is not a freezeout; it is an unverified sheet.

The ratio is descriptive: guarantee ÷ total entry fee, both as printed. It is not an expected value, not a forecast, and not a recommendation — an event with a 625× ratio is not better to play than one with 250×, because the guarantee is a floor the field may exceed and the pool depends on how many players enter. Rows whose guarantee includes bounty money, and rows whose venue names more than one starting flight, are labelled on the tournaments page so a like-for-like comparison stays like-for-like.
