Rule: the primary is the earliest non-satellite event; the fallback is marked conditional on exiting the first event (bust or late exit). An impossible sequence (two events both playable from the start) is never presented as a plan — conflicting rows land in the overlap-flag block instead. Plausible durations are planning estimates (day events ≈ 8 h, evening ≈ 5.5 h, turbo ≈ 5 h, satellite ≈ 4 h), not promises; they exist only to compute overlaps and must be re-checked against actual clock/structure at the table.
