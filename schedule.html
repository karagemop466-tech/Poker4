Day plan — one primary per day, conditional fallbacks, impossible sequences flagged

Plausible duration model (marked as an estimate everywhere): 20-minute-level events ≈ 4 h, 30-minute-level one-days ≈ 9 h, 40-minute multi-flight day-1s ≈ 10 h. Finish times are not published by the venues, so overlap verdicts are planning heuristics, not verified facts. A later event is a conditional fallback only: you can play it if you bust or exit the primary before its registration closes.
