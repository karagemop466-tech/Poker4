Tournament schedule feasibility

Day plan: what the official schedule supports

A date-only schedule cannot guarantee a full day of tournament play. Start times and late-registration cutoffs are published; finish times and re-entry policies are not consistently specified for the one-day events reviewed.

An 8 AM start is not supported by this verified series. The earliest listed future starting flight in the schedule snapshot is Nov 19 at 10:10 AM (RaceDay Stack). No 8:00 AM event is established by the sourced calendar. This statement is limited to the official Venetian series reviewed—not every poker room in Las Vegas.

Daytime option · 10:10–11:10 AM startNot single-entry verified

The lower-cost one-day listings include $200 RaceDay Stack on Nov 19–21 at 10:10 AM; the $200 NLH one-day event on Nov 22 at 11:10 AM; and $300–$600 scheduled daily NLH candidates at 11:10 AM on selected dates. Exact buy-ins, guarantees, and policy status are in the tournament table.

For RaceDay, the linked structure lists registration closing after six levels and a break (approximately 12:25 PM), with 20-minute early levels then 15-minute levels. It does not set a finishing time or state whether re-entry is permitted. Official $200 RaceDay structure ↗.

Late afternoon/evening option · 5:10 PMCould overlap

Selected scheduled days also include a 5:10 PM bounty tournament. The published start is not proof that a daytime event will have ended. A player still alive at 5:10 PM cannot be assumed able to enter both events; a bust-out time is unknown.

Example: $200 NLH Bounty, Event #9 on Nov 24, 5:10 PM; $8,000 guarantee including bounty amounts. Official calendar ↗ · Official bounty structure ↗.

Practical schedule recommendation

  1. Do not book a vacation around an assumption of 8 AM events. Ask the venue whether any unpublished or newly added events start earlier.
  2. Choose one daytime tournament per date as the primary commitment. Keep the evening tournament as an optional second entry only if the first event ends/bust-out timing permits and entry rules have been confirmed.
  3. Do not list a multi-flight event as several independent opportunities without checking entry limits: e.g., Event #7’s PDF explicitly prohibits a Day-2 bagger from entering a later flight, while it does not resolve re-entry before bagging.
  4. After booking dates, recheck the event calendar and structures because the operator reserves the right to change/cancel tournaments.

Overlap check: primary event + conditional fallback per day

Where a day lists both a morning and an afternoon/evening event, the morning event is the primary commitment and the later event is a conditional fallback — playable only if the primary event ends or the player busts out early enough. No day should be booked as an impossible sequence of two confirmed entries.
