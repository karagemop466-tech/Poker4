The three things this site changes about the plan

  1. The single-entry list is empty on purpose. No official sheet in the snapshot restricts a player to one entry. Two rooms positively price a second entry (Venetian Valuetown: “all subsequent buy in's after the initial buy in will be $200”; Wynn: 67 of 69 event sheets print “unlimited re-entry”, the other two print a one-plus-one cap). Those rows are published, quoted and kept in their own section rather than relabelled as freezeouts.
  2. The highest confirmed guarantee-to-entry ratio is $800 No Limit Hold'Em 1A — $500,000 guaranteed against a $800 entry (625.0×) — and the 72 ranked rows run from that down to $200 entries. The largest printed guarantee in the snapshot is $2,200 No Limit Hold'Em 1A Pokeratlas Championship Title Event: $1,000,000 on a $2,200 entry, from The Venetian Poker Room. Both are printed guarantees against a printed entry fee; neither is a forecast, and every row that offers a second way in is excluded from this ranking on purpose.
  3. The trip cost is dominated by the hotel, not the entries. A 6-night stay at the Venetian's own published poker rate ($119–$209 a night plus the $55 resort fee, both taxed at 13.38%) comes to about $1,184–$1,796 before a single buy-in, while the median ranked entry is $400. That is why the calculator takes nights first and why the hotel table is ordered by true cost per night rather than by distance — and why a dated checkout total from the booking calendar, not a marketing “from” price, is the number to hold the room against.

The finding that changes the plan

0 of 94 Venetian calendar rows can be labelled “single-entry confirmed”, and the same holds for every Wynn, Bellagio and Caesars row: all 253 published rows are unverified on that one question. The sheets state buy-in, fee split, guarantee, stack and level schedule — and then say nothing about whether one entry or several are allowed. Omission is not confirmation, so no row is quietly treated as a freezeout. Where a sheet does speak about extra money — the Venetian Valuetown sheet's “all subsequent buy in's after the initial buy in will be $200”, Wynn printing “unlimited re-entry” on 67 of its 69 event sheets, Bellagio's “buy-in and re-enter through the end of level 8” — that is evidence of the opposite, and those rows sit in their own section.
