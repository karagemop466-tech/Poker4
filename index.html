Planning constraints that change the answer

No 8 AM start found

The reviewed Venetian event calendar’s earliest upcoming start is 10:10 AM. A strict 8 AM tournament schedule is not available in this verified set.

“Play all day” is not guaranteed

Events may overlap or continue for an unknown duration. A 5:10 PM start cannot be treated as a second same-day entry unless the earlier event ends or the player busts in time.

Hotel total needs travel dates

Room rates vary by dates and availability. A Vegas trip date, number of nights, guests, and acceptable nightly budget are essential for a true stay total.
