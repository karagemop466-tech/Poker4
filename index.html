The finding that changes the plan

0 of 94 Venetian calendar rows can be labelled “single-entry confirmed”. The room's 38 published structure sheets state the buy-in, the fee split, the guarantee, the stack and the level schedule — and say nothing about whether one entry or several are allowed. Omission is not confirmation, so every row stays unverified rather than being quietly treated as a freezeout. Where a sheet does speak about extra money — the Valuetown sheet's “all subsequent buy in's after the initial buy in will be $200”, the $580 pre-registration rate against a $600 list price on Event 59 — that is evidence of the opposite, and those events sit in their own section.

How this stays current

All 64 official URLs are re-fetched on a schedule at 06:00 Las Vegas. The printed calendar PDF and every linked structure sheet are parsed field by field; a content hash detects cosmetic changes and only a value difference is written to the change log and flagged on the affected rows, so a re-render of the same page never looks like a schedule change. Each row carries its own “checked” date.
