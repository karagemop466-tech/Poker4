Best value single-entry tournaments

Venetian DeepStack Extravaganza IV $200 NLH single-day events (RaceDay Stack Nov 19–21, bounty events Nov 24–27, Dec 20–23) at $200 buy-in with $8,000–$10,000 guarantees — the lowest-cost structured events on the Strip right now. $300–$600 single-day events carry $10K–$30K guarantees.

Source: Venetian official schedule (S1). Single-entry status unverified — check each event's structure sheet.

Biggest guarantees

WPT Prime Championship at Wynn/Encore — $5,000,000 guarantee, $1,100 buy-in, Dec 5–14. Super Gold Million — $3,000,000 guarantee, $400 buy-in, Dec 10–14. Venetian DSE IV overall — $3.6M+ across 64 events.

Sources: Wynn official (S2), WPT schedule (S3), PokerFuse (S5). Multi-flight — single-entry status unverified.

Where to stay

Venetian/Palazzo is on-site at the DSE IV venue but costs ~$226+/night all-in (example Monday receipt incl. tax). Park MGM (~$157–$230 midweek all-in) is next to ARIA via free tram and ~20–22 min walk to Venetian and Mandalay Bay. Luxor/Excalibur are budget options connected to Mandalay Bay indoors/tram.

True cost = base + resort fee + 13.38% tax + parking. Rates for specific Nov/Dec 2026 dates unverified.
