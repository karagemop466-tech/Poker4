Executive summary

The Las Vegas Strip live-poker tournament schedule for Nov 16 – Dec 23, 2026 has been collected, organized and verified against official venue sources only: the full 64-event Venetian DeepStack Extravaganza IV schedule (93 rows with flights and restarts), the Wynn daily tournaments and the Wynn WPT World Championship festival (Nov 27 – Dec 21), Bellagio's weekly tournaments and Caesars Palace dailies. Hotels are captured with mandatory resort fees, tax, parking and cancellation terms, and rates are stored as a dated log, not single values. The headline finding is uncomfortable but verified: not one event anywhere on the Strip can currently be confirmed as single-entry / freezeout from an official document — every structure sheet either prices additional buy-ins (Venetian Valuetown, Wynn, Bellagio, Caesars) or is silent, and silence is not confirmation.

Read this before using the schedule
  • Zero confirmed freezeouts. All 37 Venetian DSE IV structure sheets, the Wynn daily structures, the Bellagio weekly PDFs and the Caesars flyer were read line by line. The "single-entry confirmed" filter on the Tournaments page therefore returns an empty set by design — showing anything else would be a hallucination. See Flagged F1 and Q3.
  • Guarantee ÷ entry-fee ratios are descriptive, not forecasts. Bounty-event guarantees include bounty money at Venetian (official sheets say so), so those ratios are not comparable with pure prize pools and are labeled separately.
  • 8:00 AM starts do not exist in any verified schedule. Earliest verified start is 9:10 AM (Venetian day-2 restarts) or 10:00 AM (Caesars). The morning gap is a data gap, shown honestly on the Schedule page.
  • Blank + flagged beats guessed. Bellagio's Saturday buy-in conflicts across three official figures ($1,100 / $1,000 / $600) and is left blank. Resort fees for Caesars/MGM properties are unverified and left blank. Walking times are unverified except Venetian/Palazzo.

How to use this site

  • Tournaments — master table with search, date/venue filters, the confirmed-single-entry toggle, and the brief-mandated sections: single-entry candidates, multi-entry (spending advantage), bounty/PKO, satellites. Ratio ranking appears with caveats per row.
  • Schedule & day plan — pick a date; get one primary event plus a conditional fallback (never an impossible sequence), overlap flags from start times, late-reg cutoffs and plausible durations, and the 8 AM goal gap.
  • Hotels — true nightly cost = rate + mandatory resort fee + 13.38% tax, dated rate logs, parking, cancellation deadlines, and the ≤30-minute walk filter (works only on verified walking times).
  • Trip calculator — entries × buy-ins + nights × true hotel cost, every figure traceable to a dated capture.
  • Venetian / Wynn / Other venues — one page per venue with its events and nearby hotels.
