Suggestions for improving the site (choose before implementation)

These are recommendations only. No external APIs, scraping, alerts, or scoring features have been implemented. Each path can be reviewed before selecting it.

Suggested first

1 · Add an official rule-verification status

Store an explicit field for entry cap, re-entry/rebuy/add-on permission, re-entry deadline, and last-checked timestamp, sourced to the rules PDF or direct written operator confirmation. Keep a tournament out of the qualifying list unless all required fields are explicit.

Decision benefit: resolves the biggest blocker to the single-entry goal. Tradeoff: requires manual operator confirmation when rules are absent or PDFs change.

Suggested second

2 · Add date-specific hotel quote comparison

Compare the actual official checkout total for identical dates and occupancy: room, resort fee, tax, parking/other unavoidable charges, deposit, cancellation rule, and poker-rate qualification. Show a per-night average and full-stay total separately.

Decision benefit: replaces starting-rate estimates with spendable totals. Tradeoff: rates are perishable and should be timestamped; needs travel dates.

Suggested later

3 · Add schedule-fit and overlap view

Calendar view with start, late-registration cutoff, known estimated end range (only when operator sources provide it), and an explicit “overlap uncertain” warning. Never auto-assume that a player can enter two same-day events.

Decision benefit: estimates maximum playable volume. Tradeoff: finish-time forecasts are variable and must stay marked estimates.

Optional

4 · Add historical pool context

For completed events, use official results pages to calculate entrant counts, actual prize pools, places paid, and buy-in fee percentages. Keep future guaranteed pools separate.

Decision benefit: helps compare actual history. Tradeoff: historical field sizes do not guarantee future participation or EV.

Optional

5 · Add location and access verification

Collect official venue addresses, property maps, and independently checked pedestrian routes; capture measurement method, route distance, and walking time in separate fields.

Decision benefit: checks the 30-minute walk constraint. Tradeoff: resort layouts and construction alter routes; refresh before travel.

Not recommended yet

6 · Automated web scraping / recommendation score

Do not add an opaque “best event” score or scrape booking pages until source parsing, change detection, licensing, and explicit re-entry semantics are reliable.

Decision benefit: could reduce refresh work. Risk: stale/ambiguous rules could silently violate the user's cost-control requirement.

Decision order

  1. Rules first: confirm strictly one entry, no re-entry/rebuy/add-on, and no paid stack/advantage mechanic.
  2. Dates second: identify the date window to filter events and obtain a real hotel quote.
  3. Schedule third: rule out overlapping starts and decide how many same-day entries are possible.
  4. Value fourth: compare buy-in, house fee, guaranteed pool, actual field/payout history, and the player's own budget—not guarantee alone.
  5. Book last: use official rate terms, calculate full-stay total, and verify walking route if not staying at the host.
All suggestions are proposals, not implemented changes. For current known gaps, see Flagged for Review.
