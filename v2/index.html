The single biggest finding: you cannot yet prove any Strip tournament is single-entry. The Venetian's own 2026 structure sheets state a late-registration window and nothing else about entry. They do not say “one entry only”, and they do not say “re-entry allowed”. Prior Venetian sheets from 2023–2024 did print “Registration and Re-Entry is allowed”, which shows the silence is meaningful and not a formatting habit. The only official single-entry statement found anywhere on the Strip is the WPT's own release for the $10,400 WPT World Championship at Wynn: “a single entry per starting flight” — and with three flights that still allows three entries in total. The question that decides everything

  1. Which exact dates are you travelling? Rates are date-specific. Without dates every hotel total is a "from" figure. The site therefore computes with your inputs rather than inventing a number.
  2. Is a single entry per flight acceptable, or must it be one entry for the whole event? This flips the entire ranking: if per-flight is acceptable, the WPT $10,400 is the only official single-entry-per-flight event found; if not, nothing on the Strip qualifies on current evidence.
  3. Will you call the poker room to get the entry rule in writing? That is the one action that converts the whole Venetian list from "unverified" to usable.
