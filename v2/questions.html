Questions that must be answered before a decision

Every question is split three ways: the best answer the current official data supports, why the answer matters to a booking, and the specific action that would settle it. 7 of 22 have a real answer today; the rest are open and are the reason the ranking stops where it does.

The five that actually gate a purchase.
  1. Are your travel dates fixed? (Every hotel number is date-specific.)
  2. Is “single entry per starting flight” acceptable, or must it be one entry for the whole event?
  3. Will the Venetian Poker Room put its re-entry rule in writing for the specific event numbers?
  4. Will you meet the poker-rate play requirement (6 h cash or a $300+ buy-in per night)?
  5. Can you walk the actual route — rooms to poker room — in under 30 minutes on the day?

How is the true nightly cost computed? answered

Answer from this dataset: (room rate + resort fee) × (1 + 13.38%), plus parking if driving. 13.38% is printed on The Venetian's own hotel terms; for the other properties it is the same Clark County rate applied as a labelled assumption.

Why it matters: Makes totals comparable across properties.

What would settle it: Settled as a formula; the inputs are the open part.
