Can you play “all day”? Here is the honest answer.

The user asked for a schedule that starts at 08:00 and runs all day. No official Strip schedule in this dataset starts at 08:00. The earliest verified start is 9:10 AM (Venetian satellite #46, Dec 12), and the earliest main-event start is 10:10 AM. This page instead gives each day one primary event and, where a second event exists, a conditional fallback — never an impossible sequence.

Why “fallback” and not “both”. A poker tournament has no published finish time. The estimator below uses the event's own structure: the time until registration closes, doubled, plus one hour of breaks — a conservative proxy for reaching the money. Two events only count as playable in sequence if the gap between their start times exceeds that estimate. This is a planning heuristic derived from the official structure, not a published finish time, and it is labelled as such everywhere.

What makes a “play all day” trip possible

  1. Morning slot. Venetian 10:10 AM main events exist on Nov 19–21 (RaceDay), Dec 2 and Dec 8 (Seniors, 50+ only), Dec 9 (#35 tag team, women/women or women/men teams), Dec 10 (LIPS Ladies — women only), Dec 13/14 (Seniors), Dec 17–19 (#56 Day 1 flights) and Dec 12 (#47 10:10 AM). For a male player under 50 the practical morning starts are Nov 19–21, Dec 9's tag team (needs a female teammate), and the Dec 17–19 $1,100 flights.
  2. Evening slot. The 5:10 PM events are one-day bounty tournaments with a 9:40 PM late-registration cut-off. A 10:10 AM start plus a plausible finish makes the 5:10 PM event a real fallback only if you bust early — which is exactly what the conditional label means.
  3. Return days. 11 calendar rows are Day-2 restarts or final tables. If you survive, you must be back in the room; a trip cannot be planned as one event per calendar day without accounting for those.

Wynn alternative

Wynn runs dailies at 12:00 PM and 6:00 PM with late registration to about 4:30 PM (Wynn daily terms). During the WPT World Championship (Nov 27 – Dec 21) the dailies are suspended (Wynn poker page), so a Wynn “all day” only works inside that window via the WPT schedule itself — see the WPT row list.
