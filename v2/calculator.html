Worked example, with every term visible

Plan: 4 Venetian entries at $300, 5 nights at The Venetian's published poker-rate floor.

Entries   4 × $300 = $1,200
True night ($119 + $55) × 1.1338 = $197.28
Hotel     5 × $197.28 = $986.42
Parking   waived if you play 3+ hours per day (else 5 × $20 = $100)
Total    $2,186.42 before food, travel and the incidental hold.

The play requirement matters twice here: the poker rate itself needs 6 hours of cash play or a $300+ buy-in per night, and free parking needs 3 hours of play. Four $300 tournaments over five nights satisfies both on paper — but the rate reverts to the prevailing rate for any night you miss.

What the calculator still cannot tell you

  • Whether your chosen event actually allows the number of entries you typed. For the Venetian, that is unresolved (see Flagged).
  • Your real room rate, until you open the booking page for your exact dates and read the checkout total.
  • Food, travel to Las Vegas, tips, and the $150/night incidental hold.
