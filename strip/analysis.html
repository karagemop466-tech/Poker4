Key findings

  • Venetian DeepStack Extravaganza IV (Nov 16 to Dec 23, 2026) is the largest on-Strip series in the official data with 74 single-entry candidate rows, including many one-day events at $200 to $600 with $8K to $40K guarantees.
  • Wynn's WPT World Championship (Nov 27 to Dec 21, 2026) lists 89 candidate rows. The biggest guarantees are Day 1 flights of $400 to $3,000 entries. The $10,400 main event lists a $1,000,000 add to the prize pool from ClubWPT Gold, which the source says is not available in Nevada.
  • Wynn's Signature Series (Jan 11 to 24, 2027) lists 31 candidate rows. Its structure sheet is still 'Coming Soon' on the official site.
  • Caesars Palace's daily $100 and $160 events are among the lowest-cost official listings in our data, but they carry an optional add-on and re-entry, so they sit on the separate page.
  • Earliest single-entry start time of day in the official data: 10:00. Nothing starts at 8 or 9 AM, so the 8 AM all-day plan is not supported by the official listings.
  • 19 candidate rows carry a note that needs confirmation (conflicting or ambiguous source text). These are marked in the table.
