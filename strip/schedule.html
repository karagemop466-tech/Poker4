Day schedule and the 8 AM question

A practical view of the official start times. It shows where an all-day plan breaks down.

Can you play all day from 8:00 AM? Not from the verified listings. The earliest single-entry candidate in the official data starts at 10:00 (Wynn Las Vegas (WPT), Thu Dec 10, 2026); the Venetian earliest is 10:10. No listed event starts at 8:00 or 9:00 AM. Event lengths are not published, so overlaps cannot be proven safe.
