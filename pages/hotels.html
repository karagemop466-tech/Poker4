Hotel tableWalking distance to Strip poker venues — true cost per night

Cost and quality are higher priority than distance, but distance must be reasonable: ≤ 30 minutes walking (~1.5 miles) of a given tournament venue. Walking times are approximate estimates from resort addresses (~3 mph), not quoted from a source.

How "true cost per night" is computed

True cost = base room rate + mandatory resort fee + 13.38% Clark County lodging tax (applies to room + fee) + self-parking (if you drive). Since May 2025, advertised Strip prices must include the resort fee (FTC rule); tax and parking are added at checkout. Example (Venetian Monday receipt, H3): base $127.20 + resort fee $55.00 = $182.20; + 13.38% tax $24.38 = $206.58; + self-parking $20.00 = $226.58 all-in for one night.

Recommended bases

  • Best single-tournament base: stay at the venue hotel — Venetian (DSE IV), Wynn/Encore (WPT), ARIA (ARIA Poker Open), Mandalay Bay (Mandalay Bay poker room), or Planet Hollywood (PH poker room). Zero walk time; pay the on-site resort fee.
  • Best value central base: Park MGM — mid-range quality, ~$157–$230 midweek all-in, free tram to ARIA, ~20–22 min walk to Venetian and Mandalay Bay. Wynn is ~36 min (just over the 30-min guideline).
  • Best budget base: Luxor or Excalibur by Mandalay Bay — indoor walkway/free tram, $45–$51 resort fee + tax, but budget property quality.
  • Money-saving: MGM Rewards Gold+ waives resort fees at ARIA, Bellagio, Cosmopolitan, Mandalay Bay, Park MGM, MGM Grand, Wynn/Encore (up to two rooms, booked direct). Caesars Rewards Platinum+ gets free self-parking at Caesars Strip properties (Venetian/Palazzo, Caesars, Planet Hollywood).

Walking-distance sanity check

  • Wynn ↔ Venetian: ~1.0 mile ≈ 20 min — reasonable.
  • Venetian ↔ ARIA: ~0.7 mile ≈ 15 min — reasonable.
  • ARIA ↔ Mandalay Bay: ~1.1 miles ≈ 22 min — reasonable.
  • Wynn ↔ Mandalay Bay: ~2.8 miles ≈ 55 min — NOT reasonable. Do not plan to walk between these two; choose a base at one end or use rideshare.
