Table BSpending-more-for-an-advantage tournaments — kept separate

Per the request, tournaments where a player can gain an advantage by spending more money — unlimited re-entry, rebuys, add-ons, or buying multiple flights — are listed here and not in the single-entry main list. Sources and verification status per row.

What separates Table B from Table A

  • Re-entry — you bust out and pay another buy-in to re-enter. That is a second (or third…) entry bought with more money.
  • Add-on — during the tournament you can pay to add chips to your stack, a direct in-game advantage through spending more (e.g., MGM Grand "Grand Add On").
  • Multi-flight — not re-entry per se, but registering for several starting flights is buying multiple entries. Strict single-entry players should play one flight only.

Strategy note

If your goal is strictly "one entry per tournament," Table B is not for you — except possibly WPT/Venetian multi-flight events if you play exactly one flight and that flight itself allows no re-entry (still needs structure-sheet confirmation). If minimizing cost matters more than single-entry purity, Horseshoe's $100 daily tournaments are the cheapest recurring action on the Strip, but their unlimited re-entry structure is exactly the "spend more for another shot" model this request asked to separate.
