2. Best single-entry tournaments (Table A)Lowest cost to enter, with guarantees

  • Best value: Venetian $200 NLH single-day events — RaceDay Stack Nov 19–21 ($200), bounty events Nov 24–27 and Dec 20–23 ($200, $8K GTD), $200 NLH Nov 22. Lowest buy-ins with listed guarantees on the Strip right now.

All "single-entry" markings are unverified until each event's structure sheet confirms no re-entry within the day/flight. See Table A (pages/tournaments.html).

Mid-range; free tram to ARIA; ~22 min walk to Venetian; ~20 min to Mandalay Bay; Wynn ~36 min (just over guideline). Resort fee $50+tax ($56.69).
Luxor / Excalibur — budget by Mandalay Bay
~$106–$190+
Budget; indoor walkway (Luxor) or free tram (Excalibur) to Mandalay Bay; resort fee $45–$51+tax. Rates not confirmed for Nov/Dec 2026.
  • On-site venue hotels: Wynn/Encore (WPT), ARIA (ARIA Poker Open), Mandalay Bay, Planet Hollywood — zero walk time; all carry $50–$55+tax resort fees (fees tier-verified, rates not).
  • MGM Rewards Gold+ waives resort fees at MGM properties (ARIA, Bellagio, Cosmopolitan, Mandalay Bay, Park MGM, Wynn/Encore, booked direct). Caesars Rewards Platinum+ = free self-parking at Caesars Strip properties (Venetian/Palazzo, Caesars, Planet Hollywood).
  • Not walkable together: Wynn and Mandalay Bay are ~2.8 miles (~55 min) apart — do not base yourself expecting to walk between them.

7. Bottom lineFor a single-entry-focused trip

Suggested planning path

  1. Pick dates within Nov 16 – Dec 23, 2026.
  2. Base at the Venetian (DSE IV on-site) for the lowest-cost single-entry action ($200 single-day events), or Park MGM for a cheaper central base within walking distance of Venetian/ARIA/Mandalay Bay.
  3. Play Venetian $200 single-day events in the morning; only add an evening event if you bust early.
  4. Use Horseshoe dailies only if you accept unlimited re-entry (Table B).
  5. Confirm every event's structure sheet and every hotel's all-in rate before booking — nothing here is a guarantee.
