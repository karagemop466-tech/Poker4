How to read Table A

  • Buy-in / Guarantee: taken from the official schedules. A "—" means the value was not listed and was left blank (never inferred).
  • Single-day events (marked "1Day") have no flights in the schedule — the closest thing to single-entry on offer, but re-entry within the day still needs structure-sheet confirmation.
  • Multi-flight events let you register for more than one starting flight — that is buying multiple entries, which conflicts with a strict single-entry goal.
  • The full Venetian DSE IV grid (64 events, Nov 16 – Dec 23, $3.6M+ GTD) and WPT festival grid are linked in the Source column.
