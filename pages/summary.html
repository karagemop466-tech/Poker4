1Recommended plan

  • Tournament focus: Venetian DeepStack Extravaganza IV (Nov 16 – Dec 23, 2026) — 64 events, $3.6M+ guaranteed, on the Strip. Concentrate on the $200–$300 1-Day events for the lowest buy-in with real guarantees; step up to $400–$600 events ($75K–$500K GTD) for bigger fields.
  • Series play: WPT World Championship at Wynn (Nov 27 – Dec 21, 2026) — Super Gold Million $400 with $3,000,000 GTD (Dec 10–12) and WPT Prime $1,100 with $5,000,000 GTD (Dec 5–12). These are multi-flight events where re-entry across Day 1 flights is generally possible — listed in the separate spend-more section; confirm on wynnpoker.com structure sheets.
  • Wynn daily fallback (when not in series): $200 noon ($10K GTD Mon–Thu, $25K GTD Sat–Sun) and $160 nightly 6 PM ($10K GTD) — official Wynn poster. Suspended Nov 27–Dec 21.
  • Hotel: stay central — Caesars Palace, The LINQ, Flamingo/Harrah's, or on-site at The Venetian/Wynn — so Venetian, Wynn, and Aria are within ~10–28 minutes walking. Caesars Palace is the only hotel with an official verified rate (Dec 11–13: $595/$595/$126). Trump International (next to Wynn) advertises no resort fee — unverified, to be confirmed.

4What must be confirmed before any booking or playing decision

  • Re-entry policy for every tournament (official structure sheets) — most daily tournaments and DSE multi-flight events allow re-entry, which violates "single entry only."
  • Hotel rates and resort fees directly on each hotel's official site (official booking engines did not render live rates in this research except Caesars Palace).
  • Walking times from hotel to venue on Google Maps (estimates only here).
  • Tournament cancellations/schedule changes — call poker rooms (e.g., Wynn (702) 770-7654; Venetian poker room via venetianlasvegas.com).
