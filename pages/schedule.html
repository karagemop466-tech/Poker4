Schedule planPlaying live tournaments all day on the Strip

No 8 AM start exists — "play all day starting at 8 AM" is not supportable

No tournament in the research window (Nov 16 – Dec 23, 2026) begins at 8:00 AM. The earliest confirmed starts are 10:10 AM at the Venetian (DSE RaceDay Stack and other events) and 11:00 AM at Wynn/Horseshoe. Any plan advertising an 8 AM tournament start would be invented — it is left blank instead. The sample plans below start at the earliest confirmed time and run as late as the schedule allows.

Tournaments overlap — you usually cannot play two at once

Most morning events run several hours, so a same-day "morning tournament + evening tournament" plan only works if you bust out of the morning event early (or skip Day 2/restarts). If you make Day 2 of a multi-flight event, that locks you into another day. These samples assume you bust early and re-plan the afternoon — they are illustrations, not guaranteed itineraries.
