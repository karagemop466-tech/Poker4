Caesars Palace Poker Room

3570 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89109 · Caesars Palace

coverage: No structure sheet is published. The venue's own 2026 daily flyer PDF prints four daily NLH slots events listed: 4 confirmed single-entry: 0 hotels in range: 0

10 AM $100, 2 PM $160, 6 PM $100, 9 PM $160 — each with 20,000 chips, 20-minute levels and a $1,000 guarantee, plus the fee splits ($70/$30 and $115/$45), the $50 add-on split ($45/$5), registration closing at the start of level 7, and a re-entry rule (re-enter within the first break after elimination). The explore page's blanket 'every event carries a $100 buy-in' sentence conflicts with the flyer's $160 slots — flag F25 records the conflict and the rows follow the dedicated, dated flyer. Rows stay excluded from ranking because the slate prints no calendar dates.

Rows preserved from the earlier snapshot are labelled 'needs re-verification' rather than deleted.
